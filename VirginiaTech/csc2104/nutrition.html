One of my favorite comfort foods is a classic beef chilli. It's easy and very quick to make, has few cheap ingredients, and tastes great! It may not be the most nutritionally rounded, but still provides some good protien. It consists of ground beef, onion, tomatoes, kidney beans, beef broth, and lots of spice! The spices can be changed depending on your taste, but I like to use a lot of chilli powder, a good amount of garlic powder and cumin, a small amount of powdered thyme, paprika, and onion powder, and a tiny amount of salt, pepper, and cayenne powder. I also like to use a fair bit of sugar to balance out the heat and savory nature of the dish. After cooking, you can add shreeded cheese on top, green onions, or even sour cream.
