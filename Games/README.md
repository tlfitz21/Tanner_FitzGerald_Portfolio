1.) Bring Your Parasite to Work Day
      -This was a game made in 48 hours by myself and David O'Regan. It was for the Virginia Tech Game Development Club Fall 2026 Game Jam
      -The theme for the game Jam was Odd Jobs
      -Our Game features you playing as an alien who has taken on the job of taking over earth by defeating the president of the USA. you must
      infect various creatures, jumping from host to host by doing their everyday jobs, until you work your way from a farm animal, to the president.
      -To play, go into the BringYourParasiteToWorkDayBuild folder and run the game-jam-2026.exe file
